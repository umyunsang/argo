\begin{abstract}
Most LLM agents execute objectives, methods, and evaluation criteria supplied by
a human.  A long-horizon research agent must instead formulate falsifiable
hypotheses, select resources and controls, preserve evidence across experiments,
and revise its research direction without rewriting answered protocols.  This
thesis studies a context-graph-driven LLM-agent harness that represents
hypotheses, decisions, experiments, findings, and claims as persistent linked
state and reopens retrieval from positive, null, negative, and execution-failure
outcomes.

A root-agent review of five direct experimental analogues changed the prospective
evaluation.  SCOPE reports that search access alone is not reliably beneficial
for experimental design, while Arbor indicates that hierarchy without propagated
insight is insufficient.  The first empirical study therefore uses a 2$\times$2
factorial design crossing dynamic retrieval with a stage-isolated decision and
evidence protocol.

The study did not reach a treatment effect.  It reached the prior question of
whether its own instruments can be trusted, and the reported result is that
several could not.  A 16-episode instrument pilot, a 32-episode variance block
and a verification pass producing 192 element judgements were executed.  The
first primary endpoint, reference-anchored cue coverage, was falsified by its own
preregistered adversarial test at a false-positive rate of 0.969 and replaced by
retrieval plus verification.  A cost instrument was later found to understate one
episode by a factor of 14.4 while its own fixture asserted the wrong behaviour, so
every cost figure it had produced was voided and re-measured.  A mutation audit of
the nine measuring instruments found four fault classes that no test detected,
including one that let a fail-closed sandbox report a workspace admissible while a
probe had fired.  Of 28 recorded decisions, every one carries a falsifier written
before its result, and revisions are reported rather than quietly applied.

This thesis therefore reports no local efficacy estimate, harness-performance
result, or field-instance outcome.  Judged scoring remains inadmissible until a
human-anchored calibration set exists, and the contribution is the instrument
discipline and its recorded failures rather than a measured effect.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

A capable language model is only one part of a long-horizon research agent.
Persistent context, action grounded in external tools, retrieval, coordination,
and learning from prior trials are also required.  ReAct interleaves reasoning
and action, MemGPT and Recursive Language Models externalize working context, and
AutoGen composes multiple agents, humans, and tools \citep{react2023,memgpt2023,
rlm2025,autogen2023}.  These mechanisms make long-running agents possible, but
they do not by themselves make the agent responsible for designing and revising
an empirical research program.

The scientific problem addressed in this thesis is narrower than general agent
capability and stricter than task completion.  An autonomous empirical agent
must decide what question is worth asking, distinguish competing mechanisms,
freeze a valid comparison before seeing its outcome, preserve the identity of
the code and artifacts that produced the outcome, and limit every conclusion to
the population and protocol actually measured.  It must also expose the points
at which human authority remains decisive.  A run that produces a favorable metric
without these properties may be useful engineering, but it is not sufficient
evidence for a scientific claim.

\subsection{Study scope and objective}

The proposed system is studied as a unified harnessed LLM-agent system rather than a collection
of independent tools.  Its research loop connects persistent computation,
dynamic literature retrieval, a typed context graph, hypothesis and method
selection, experiment execution, evidence assimilation, controlled harness
adaptation, and manuscript generation.  The central question is whether wiring
these capabilities into one persistent system improves the continuity, validity,
and efficiency of autonomous empirical research.

The evaluation is staged so that research-design validity is tested before
end-to-end harness optimization.  Study A isolates the effects of structured
research state and retrieval on paper-derived hidden experimental-design tasks.
A later Study B may evaluate flat, hierarchical, and insight-propagating artifact
optimization, but only after Study A establishes an executable and independently
scored treatment.  Development, instrument-pilot, and confirmatory tasks remain
disjoint.  A result that changes the method becomes development evidence for its
descendant rather than confirmation of the revised system.

Staging in this order has a consequence that shapes what this thesis can
currently claim.  Before an effect can be estimated, the instrument that would
estimate it must itself be admissible, and evaluating a generative system is a
measurement problem in its own right \citep{genaimeasurement2025}.  The work
reported here reached that boundary and stopped at it: the first scoring
instrument was falsified by its own preregistered test, replaced, and the
replacement was measured for stability before any label was spent.  No treatment
effect is claimed anywhere in this thesis.

That boundary is not incidental to a system that studies itself.  When one agent
controls both the object being optimized and the verifier that scores it,
self-assigned scores can stay high while real performance does not
\citep{verifiergap2026}, and an agent that edits its own harness can manufacture
illusory gains unless the modification is audited \citep{harnesstamper2026}.
Self-evolving loops also presuppose a reliable metric that frequently does not
exist yet \citep{whogrades2026}.  The design therefore separates the generating
agent from the verifying model family, keeps the scoring anchor in evaluator-owned
files, and records every instrument change as a dated decision with a falsifier,
so that a reader can trace where each number came from rather than trusting the
system that produced it \citep{auditablewriting2026}.

The current paper-research context graph is a living design instrument, not a
runtime claim.  It records why the original bundled comparison was revised, the
prior evidence supporting each constituent, and the hidden-task, independent-
scoring, and fixed-runner gaps that block launch.  No autonomous-research
efficacy estimate or field-instance outcome is available in the present
manuscript.

\subsection{Research questions}

\paragraph{RQ1: Structured research state.}
Under matched model, task, inference, tool-call, and time budgets, does a
stage-isolated decision and evidence graph increase fatal-error-free experimental-
design completion relative to free-form planning and ordinary notes?

\paragraph{RQ2: Retrieval and structure interaction.}
What is the main effect of dynamic literature and method retrieval, and does
retrieval contribute more when resource selection is separated from protocol
construction and every source is linked to a decision change or explicit no-
change record?

\paragraph{RQ3: Measurement validity.}
Can hidden paper-derived tasks, deterministic redlines, condition-blind scoring,
and retrieval-trajectory diagnostics distinguish scientific design quality from
presentation quality, search volume, and self-attested evidence validity?

\paragraph{RQ4: Deferred harness optimization.}
If Study A identifies a valid research-state treatment, do a hypothesis tree,
propagated insight, and held-out admission produce more transferable artifact
improvements than a flat queue under matched budgets?

\section{Related Works}

\paragraph{Agent reasoning, memory, and orchestration.}
ReAct established an interleaved reasoning--action loop in which reasoning
updates plans and actions gather information from tools and environments
\citep{react2023}.  Reflexion stores verbal feedback in episodic memory for
subsequent trials \citep{reflexion2023}, while MemGPT and Recursive Language
Models externalize or virtualize context beyond a single model window
\citep{memgpt2023,rlm2025}.  Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines parametric
generation with dense non-parametric memory \citep{rag2020}.  AutoGen shows how
customizable agents, humans, and tools can be composed through programmable
conversation patterns \citep{autogen2023}.  Together these works provide the
reasoning, memory, retrieval, and orchestration foundations needed for a
long-running research agent, but they do not define an end-to-end R\&D process.

\paragraph{Harness functions and coding-agent architecture.}
A harness determines how an LLM receives context, executes code and tools,
persists state, delegates work, applies approval, and exposes traces.  Code as
Agent Harness provides a broad taxonomy; source-level comparison and
long-horizon studies separate permissions, compaction, audited task state,
regression obligations, and programmatic context management
\citep{codeharness2026,dive2026,longhorizon2026,loopsbench2026,scroll2026}.
These sources establish separable design axes, not a universal best harness.

\paragraph{Protocol layers and human collaboration.}
A taxonomy of nine agent protocols distinguishes counterparty, payload,
interaction state, discovery, and schema flexibility, and shows why structured
tool access is not equivalent to session-stateful peer collaboration
\citep{protocoltaxonomy2026}.  CHAP adds a proposed grammar for typed human
review, override, abstention, escalation, modes, and evidence, while explicitly
remaining a draft that lacks independent multi-implementation evaluation
\citep{chap2026}.  The method therefore treats tool, peer-agent, and human
communication as composable layers with separate authority tests.

\paragraph{Procedural skills and normative constraints.}
Operational procedures turn plans into reusable action sequences, whereas
normative constraints limit which sequences are allowed and when authority must
return to a human.  ReAct provides the basic observation-conditioned procedure;
the protocol literature adds explicit lifecycle, review, and evidence events
\citep{react2023,protocoltaxonomy2026,chap2026}.  This separation prevents a
successful procedure from being mistaken for an authorized one.

\paragraph{RAG and the agentic development stack.}
RAG is not one operation: document segmentation, sparse and dense indexing,
retrieval, reranking, context repair, generation, and citation checking can fail
independently.  Intent-aware chunk repair makes semantic fragmentation explicit;
a compute-aware scientific study reports that a generic web-trained reranker can
reduce precision; and adaptive RAG reports corpus-dependent benefits and costs
from decomposition and reflection
\citep{insemrag2026,sciret2026,adaptiverag2026}.  These mixed findings motivate
the retrieval factor and independent relevance labels rather than a presumption
that a larger pipeline is better.

\paragraph{Dynamic model and workflow routing.}
Routing can choose a model, resample, reroute, change a workflow, or assign a
specialist to a subtask.  Current studies report that the best policy varies by
task and budget, that gains can be verifier-gated, that orchestration effects
interact with the backbone, and that calibrated subtask allocation is vulnerable
to distribution shift
\citep{llmrouter2026,resamplereroute2026,controlledorchestration2026,agora2026}.
Consequently Study~A fixes one model; routing is a separate deferred quality--cost
experiment rather than an additional treatment factor.

\paragraph{Autonomous scientific research.}
The AI Scientist automates ideation, experimentation, writing, and review but
also reports code, control, and review failures \citep{aiscientist2024}.
Co-Scientist uses specialized generation, reflection, ranking, evolution, and
meta-review roles \citep{coscientist2025}; SciAgents grounds hypothesis
formation in a literature graph \citep{sciagents2024}; and EviGraph, XScientist,
and ScientistOne connect experiments and manuscripts to structured evidence
\citep{evigraph2026,xscientist2026,scientistone2026}.  These systems motivate
the autonomous research scope while leaving open how one persistent agent should
own the complete experimental design and revise it from outcomes.

\paragraph{Experimental-design and research-process evaluation.}
SCOPE evaluates high-level main, ablation, and analysis planning separately from
low-level dataset, baseline, and metric configuration; its matched evaluation
reports that naive search access is not reliably beneficial and motivates stage
isolation and typed operations \citep{scope2026}.  ResearchClawBench withholds
paper-derived targets and uses expert-weighted rubrics across 40 dry-lab tasks,
but its authors note that final-report scoring does not cover every research step
or independently establish novel discovery \citep{researchclawbench2026}.
Arbor directly ablates a flat queue, a hierarchy without propagated insight, and
a full hypothesis-tree treatment under matched access and budgets
\citep{arbor2026}.  HEP compares explicit hypothesis--evidence--belief records
with planning under the same goal, tools, and model, while identifying same-agent
evidence validation as a limitation \citep{hep2026}.  Search-trajectory analysis
further shows that evidence retrieval, evidence utilization, redundancy, and
stopping must be measured separately from search count \citep{searchdiagnosis2026}.
These findings motivate the factorial Study A design rather than the original
bundled three-condition comparison.

\paragraph{Harness adaptation and evaluation.}
Continual Harness, Reflexion, RSEA, Regimes, and Autogenesis study persistent
adaptation through memory, reflection, held-out selection, auditable history,
and versioned rollback \citep{continual2026,reflexion2023,rsea2026,
regimes2026,autogenesis2026}.  Evo-Bench and HarnessOpt-Bench isolate harness
optimization from model strength and protect held-out evaluation
\citep{evobench2026,harnessopt2026}.  A matched-budget study further reports
that harness evolution did not consistently outperform task-level discovery in
its Terminal-Bench setting \citep{rethinkingharness2026}.  This counterevidence
motivates the central experimental question: whether autonomous research
state, outcome-driven retrieval, and controlled promotion add value beyond
additional search and self-reflection.

\section{Proposed Method}

\subsection{Unit of scientific work}

The proposed method represents a consequential scientific choice as a typed decision record rather
than as an unstructured chat message.  The record binds the research objective,
assumptions, population, alternatives, intervention, control, estimand, metric,
uncertainty method, falsifier, and stopping rule before outcome visibility.  It
also links the choice to prior evidence, counterevidence, code identity, and the
comparison objects described below.  A changed design creates a descendant
record so that the rationale associated with an answered experiment is not
rewritten retrospectively.

\subsection{Measurement instruments as first-class design objects}

The design treats its scoring instruments as objects that must be validated
before use, not as fixed background machinery.  Three instruments are specified
and each carries its own falsifier.

The \emph{release sandbox} builds an episode workspace containing only released
bytes and runs fail-closed probes for withheld-content leakage, symlink escape,
environment leakage, scoring-asset reachability, and unexecuted numeric claims.
Its falsifier is that a probe never fires on a deliberately corrupted fixture.

The \emph{scoring instrument} anchors on the withheld target design of each task
and judges each required element separately.  Cue matching retrieves candidate
spans; it does not decide satisfaction, because deciding by cue was falsified.
Its falsifier is that scores fail to separate artifacts an independent reader
would rank differently.

The \emph{selective evaluator} admits a judged verdict only above a confidence
threshold whose error bound is certified on blinded human-anchored labels, using
an upper confidence bound rather than an empirical rate, and it admits nothing
while uncalibrated.  Its falsifier is that errors concentrate at high confidence,
where no threshold can help.

\subsection{Nine-area architecture and validation boundary}

The target system is organized into nine separable research areas.  Separation is
necessary because the current study validates only the connected path needed to
identify structured state and retrieval; it does not treat the full architecture
as implemented or effective.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item 	extbf{Harness functions:} persistent orchestration, capability sandbox,
  independent evaluator, approval, compaction, and observability.
  \item 	extbf{Memory functions:} working context, decision/evidence graph,
  semantic knowledge, episodic experience, and consented personalized memory.
  \item 	extbf{Protocols:} typed user, peer-agent, and tool/resource exchanges
  with distinct state, identity, authority, and failure semantics.
  \item 	extbf{Skills:} operational procedures separated from normative
  constraints and decision heuristics.
  \item 	extbf{RAG engine:} versioned chunking, sparse/dense retrieval,
  independently validated reranking, bounded context repair, and evidence-bound
  generation.
  \item 	extbf{Agentic development stack:} provider-neutral agents, tools,
  protocol clients and servers, vector retrieval, workflow state, and
  multi-agent composition.
  \item 	extbf{Coding-agent harness architecture:} programmatic context,
  executable code action, persistent sessions, recursive delegation, and
  capability-bounded extension.
  \item 	extbf{Autonomous research engine:} question selection, full-source
  review, experiment design, execution receipts, claim--evidence lineage,
  deterministic validation, outcome updates, stopping, and manuscript review.
  \item 	extbf{Model and workflow routing:} task/role/depth-conditioned model,
  reasoning budget, provider capability, availability, and verifier-gated
  fallback.
\end{enumerate}

Study~A fixes the model, approval policy, context limit, workflow envelope, and
evaluator revision.  It manipulates only structured research state and dynamic
retrieval.  Personalized memory, complete protocol interoperability, multiple
vector backends, provider fallback, dynamic routing, and runtime changes remain
design-only or follow-up capabilities.  This boundary prevents a nine-area
roadmap from becoming an unsupported nine-area result claim.

\subsection{Context graph and outcome-driven retrieval}

The context graph connects research questions, prior mechanisms, candidate
methods, DecisionRecords, experiments, artifacts, results, counterevidence, and
manuscript claims.  It serves as the persistent research state shared across
literature search, code execution, result analysis, and writing.  This continuity
is intended to reduce duplicate proposals and to preserve why an experiment was
selected, not merely what score it produced.

Literature retrieval remains adaptive.  Before execution it supplies mechanisms,
methods, controls, and known failure modes.  Positive results trigger searches
for replication, transfer limits, causal alternatives, and counterevidence;
null results trigger measurement and heterogeneity analysis; negative results
trigger searches for analogous regressions and alternative methods.  Execution
failure initiates implementation or specification research but does not update
the scientific hypothesis.  Literature therefore shapes the next question
without acting as authority for a local efficacy claim.

\subsection{Decision protocol}

The decision protocol has six dimensions: delegated authority and ethical
constraints; identifiability of the proposed estimand; claim-appropriate evidence
and mechanism-distinct alternatives; explicit nuisance invariants and allowed
arm differences; information value with independent critique; and
preregistration followed by scoped assimilation.  The protocol is evaluated as
a treatment rather than assumed to be optimal.  Relevant outcomes include
invalid comparisons admitted, valid comparisons blocked, correction latency,
human intervention, compute cost, and the breadth of conclusions drawn from the
same evidence.

Recency, popularity, citation count, model confidence, or a convenient local
metric cannot establish efficacy.  A negative experiment likewise does not
close a method family outside its measured population, learner, and protocol.
Both false acceptance and false rejection are therefore treated as scientific
errors.

\subsection{Three-object comparison identity}

Protocol comparability is not equality of every hash.  A legitimate experiment
must differ in its intervention and will often differ in code or configuration.
The proposed comparison method therefore uses three linked objects for each contrast:

\begin{description}
  \item[Invariant protocol key.] The fields that define the common comparison:
  population, data roles, split, target, estimand, metric, aggregation,
  environment class, and deployment contract.  Model selector and access
  policy, base prompt, permissions, budget, evaluator revision, and the
  order/time block are included when they are nuisance variables rather than the
  intervention.
  \item[Per-arm receipt.] The exact realized identity of each arm, including
  model access, delivered prompt, permissions and tools, code, configuration,
  dependencies and resources, seed or clean-session repeat, order/time block,
  evaluator revision, and produced artifacts.
  \item[Planned-contrast manifest.] The only differences allowed between arms,
  the intervention each difference implements, and the causal label that the
  contrast may support.
\end{description}

A pair is matched when all declared nuisance invariants agree and the actual arm
differences equal the preregistered allowed differences.  An undeclared
difference makes the causal contrast invalid; a declared treatment difference
does not.  In particular, requiring complete arm records or implementation
artifacts to be identical would reject the intervention being studied.  A future implementation
may hash each canonical object for identity, but it must compare their typed
fields and planned differences rather than collapse comparability into one
full-receipt equality test.

\subsection{Persistent execution and research continuity}

Autonomous R\&D requires research state to survive beyond a single model call.
The proposed harness therefore combines persistent programmatic computation, recursive task
decomposition, recoverable context, and durable adaptation state motivated by
Recursive Language Models, MemGPT, Reflexion, and Continual Harness
\citep{rlm2025,memgpt2023,reflexion2023,continual2026}.  These mechanisms are prior work;
the research question is whether connecting them to literature, experiment, and
evidence state enables a more coherent autonomous research loop.

Durable adaptation also creates risk.  A harness can preserve a shortcut that
helps the cases that produced it while degrading other tasks.  This motivates
held-out selection, explicit version lineage, and rollback rather than treating
self-reflection as evidence that a change is safe
\citep{rsea2026,regimes2026,autogenesis2026}.

\subsection{Typed research and claim-admissibility graph}

SciAgents grounds hypothesis generation in a literature graph
\citep{sciagents2024}.  EviGraph adds typed evidence relations and dependency-
aware repair \citep{evigraph2026}; claim-aware observability makes claims and
verification records explicit \citep{claimobservability2026}; and XScientist and
ScientistOne connect research artifacts to claim-specific evidence
\citep{xscientist2026,scientistone2026}.  The proposed method uses these ideas as an operational
research state spanning objectives, prior work, mechanisms, hypotheses, methods,
experiments, artifacts, results, counterevidence, and manuscript claims.

Edges preserve the causal and evidential relationships among those objects: a
run executes one preregistered protocol, an artifact comes from one run, and a
claim cites the artifact or source span that supports its scope.  An answered
experiment remains immutable, while revised interpretations or methods become
descendants.  The graph thereby supports both continuity across research stages
and outcome-driven selection of the next search or experiment.

Evidence admissibility is a claim-type lattice rather than one scalar ladder.
Table~\ref{tab:admissibility} summarizes the minimum evidence class for each
claim.  The classes are orthogonal: satisfying one row does not promote evidence
for another row.

A discovery record may motivate a question but cannot support detailed method
or result wording.  A full paper can support what prior authors report but not
local software behavior.  Primary code can establish identity without proving a
license or efficacy claim.  A local run cannot establish an external outcome,
and an external receipt cannot identify which mechanism caused it.  Boolean
verification fields, transcript summaries, and durable harness memories do not
self-attest any row.

\subsection{Experiment execution and scientific validity}

The proposed method separates execution evidence from scientific interpretation.  The
experiment engine supplies the identity and outcome of a run, while the research
state determines whether the comparison was meaningful and what conclusion the
result supports.  A reproducible execution can still answer a poorly identified
question; conversely, a sound hypothesis is not falsified by a failed execution.
This separation allows the same research loop to use an external experiment
engine without treating engine status as scientific judgment.

\subsection{Research refinement and harness refinement}

The proposed method distinguishes two forms of adaptation.  Research refinement updates
hypotheses, methods, and scoped beliefs from admissible evidence while preserving
the identity of answered experiments.  Harness refinement changes prompts,
memory, skills, tools, or coordination policies for subsequent work.  The
separation prevents the system from making a hypothesis appear stronger by
altering the measurement process that judges it.

Held-out context selection, evaluator isolation, auditable promotion, and
versioned rollback have direct precedents \citep{rsea2026,evobench2026,
regimes2026,autogenesis2026}.  The proposed method combines these ideas with a versioned-state
noninterference hypothesis: harness changes may improve future research behavior
but may not rewrite prior results, scientific-state ancestry, or evaluators.
Matched task-level search and inaccessible confirmatory evidence are used to
separate genuine harness improvement from additional search budget
\citep{rethinkingharness2026,harnessopt2026}.

\subsection{Evidence-linked manuscript synthesis}

Manuscript generation is the final stage of the research loop rather than a
separate summarization task.  EviGraph, claim-locked reporting, claim-aware
observability, XScientist, and ScientistOne provide prior mechanisms for
graph-grounded writing, evidence-before-prose control, portable research
artifacts, and claim-specific verification \citep{evigraph2026,
claimlocked2026,claimobservability2026,xscientist2026,scientistone2026}.

The proposed method synthesizes text from the same context graph used to design and interpret
experiments.  Literature claims link to inspected source spans; empirical claims
link to run artifacts and predefined result fields; methodological claims link
to the executed protocol and implementation identity.  Unsupported or stale
edges remain visible as unresolved research state rather than being repaired by
fluent prose.  This makes writing part of evidence assimilation and allows a
result to trigger further research before a conclusion is finalized.

\subsection{Research contribution}

The contribution under study is the closed-loop research behavior of a single
harnessed agent.  The agent frames the question, searches and organizes related work,
selects hypotheses and methods, executes controlled experiments, interprets the
outcomes, searches again, improves its harness, and writes from accumulated
evidence.  The individual reasoning, memory, retrieval, multi-agent, and
adaptation mechanisms are grounded in the prior work reviewed above.  The
empirical question is whether their integration allows the agent to take
responsibility for a long-running R\&D program and produce better research and
harness outcomes than information-matched alternatives.  No aggregate
originality or performance advantage is assumed.

\subsection{Evaluation protocol}

\paragraph{Study status.}
The protocol is prospective.  Adaptive full-paper review changed the first
experiment from a bundled three-arm harness comparison to a factorial research-
design study.  The instrument pilot, development pilot, and confirmation have
not been executed.  Evaluation E5 remains blocked until hidden-task release,
independent scoring, and fixed-runner gates pass.

\subsubsection{Study A factors and conditions}

Study A crosses structured research state $S$ with dynamic retrieval $R$:

\begin{description}
  \item[C00: $S=0,R=0$.] The agent receives the released task packet and
  produces a free-form experimental plan without search or typed graph tools.
  \item[C01: $S=0,R=1$.] The same agent may retrieve literature and methods,
  but retains ordinary notes and has no stage or graph contract.
  \item[C10: $S=1,R=0$.] Retrieval is disabled.  Resource configuration,
  protocol construction, and reporting use separate contexts, while alternatives,
  rationales, falsifiers, and evidence dependencies are stored as typed state.
  \item[C11: $S=1,R=1$.] Both stage-isolated state and adaptive retrieval are
  available; each retrieval must link to a decision change or explicit no-change
  record.
\end{description}

All conditions use the same model revision, high-level task information, output
schema, 32,000-token ceiling, 12 tool-call ceiling, 45-minute wall time, clean-
session policy, machine class, and zero target-evaluation queries during
treatment.  Only the two declared factors may differ.  Search results consume
the common context and tool budget rather than adding a hidden budget.

\subsubsection{Hypotheses and outcomes}

\paragraph{H-A: structured-state main effect.}
Stage-isolated decision and evidence state increases fatal-error-free complete
design (FEC) relative to free-form planning.  FEC requires a main experiment,
ablation, analysis experiment, compatible resources, matched baselines, metrics,
and uncertainty method with no fabricated resource, metric incompatibility,
constraint violation, hidden-evidence access, post-outcome edit, or over-broad
conclusion.

\paragraph{H-B: retrieval main effect.}
No positive direction is assumed.  SCOPE directly motivates estimating whether
retrieval improves low-level configuration, leaves design unchanged, or disrupts
high-level reasoning \citep{scope2026}.

\paragraph{H-C: structure--retrieval interaction.}
Retrieval should contribute more when evidence acquisition is separated from
protocol construction and recorded as decision-relevant state.  This hypothesis
is falsified if the retrieval contrast under structure is no larger than the
retrieval contrast without structure, or if retrieval only adds searches and
citations.

Let $p_{sr}=\Pr(\mathrm{FEC}=1\mid S=s,R=r)$.  The preregistered factorial
contrasts are

\begin{align}
\Delta_S &= \tfrac12[(p_{10}-p_{00})+(p_{11}-p_{01})],\\
\Delta_R &= \tfrac12[(p_{01}-p_{00})+(p_{11}-p_{10})],\\
\Delta_{SR} &= (p_{11}-p_{10})-(p_{01}-p_{00}).
\end{align}

Secondary outcomes retain six design dimensions separately and report redline
types, retrieval and utilization gaps, productive/redundant/unproductive search
episodes, decision-changing retrievals, duplicate queries, graph errors, tokens,
tool calls, wall time, and human intervention.  Search count, citation count,
report length, and presentation quality are not efficacy endpoints.

\subsubsection{Instrument pilot and task boundary}

The minimum instrument pilot contains four paper-derived dry-lab tasks from four
task families crossed with all four conditions: 16 clean episodes.  It tests
plumbing, leakage, scoring, logging, and budget parity only; it is too small for
an efficacy claim.  A later development pilot estimates paired FEC discordance,
within-task stochastic variance, task-family correlation, and judge--human
disagreement.  Confirmatory task and repeat counts are selected by simulation
from those quantities, and all pilot tasks are excluded from confirmation.

The treatment source archive and workspace may contain released instructions,
data, and related work, but not target papers, rubrics, evaluator keys, target
hashes, or target-derived error messages.  ResearchClawBench motivates this task
projection but does not by itself establish process-level scoring or a secure
filesystem boundary \citep{researchclawbench2026}.  Failing-first probes must
reject traversal, links, inherited descriptors, environment leakage, evaluator
queries, and cache-based target access before any pilot launch.

\subsubsection{Evaluation and uncertainty}

The evaluator receives normalized outputs without condition labels after the
treatment process terminates.  Programmatic checks establish resource existence,
metric compatibility, required fields, budget identity, and forbidden leakage.
An independent rubric judge scores the six design dimensions; a human-reviewed
subset calibrates judge agreement.  HEP's same-agent evidence validation is not
accepted as an independent check \citep{hep2026}.

Retrieval trajectories are classified into retrieval and utilization gaps and
productive, redundant, and unproductive episodes following prior diagnostic
work \citep{searchdiagnosis2026}.  The confirmatory analysis uses paired
condition-label permutations within task and a hierarchy-preserving bootstrap
over task family and task.  Repeats remain nested within task and do not create
additional independent units.

\subsubsection{Deferred Study B}

Only after Study A identifies a valid research-state treatment will Study B test
flat experiment queues, hypothesis trees, and tree-plus-insight propagation on
artifact-optimization tasks.  Arbor motivates these constituents and a held-out
admission gate, but its task scope, scalar objectives, and high search cost
preclude treating its reported effects as local evidence \citep{arbor2026}.
Harness performance, rollback, contamination, transfer, and cross-lineage
noninterference remain deferred outcomes.

\subsubsection{Outcome-driven retrieval and stopping}

Positive outcomes trigger replication, transfer, causal-alternative, and
counterevidence searches.  Null outcomes trigger variance, rubric-sensitivity,
heterogeneity, and mechanism-distinct searches.  Negative outcomes trigger
searches for graph overhead, anchoring, utilization failure, and incompatible
assumptions.  Execution failures trigger implementation or specification research
only.  A retrieval line stops when new evidence does not change a hypothesis,
resource, control, metric, stopping rule, or scoped interpretation.

Two consecutive infrastructure-only failures stop and repair the same provisional
node; they do not update H-A through H-C.  Leakage, budget mismatch, scorer
identity mismatch, or target-evaluation access invalidates the entire pilot
block.  Scientific interpretation requires the complete preregistered condition
block and immutable experiment logs.

\section{Experimental Results}

No treatment effect is estimated in this work.  The executed evidence is
instrument evidence: measurements of whether the measuring apparatus itself is
admissible.  This is reported rather than withheld, because an evaluation
pipeline can lose validity stage by stage \citep{compounding2026}, because a
validity audit can fail in its own characteristic ways \citep{auditaudit2026},
and because filtering out negative findings distorts the literature that later
systems learn from \citep{negativeresults2026}.

\subsection{Executed instrument blocks}

Three blocks were executed under a fixed command with one pinned backend for
generation and a different provider family for verification.  An instrument
pilot produced 16 episodes across four tasks and four conditions.  A second
block produced 32 episodes across four unseen tasks with two repeats per cell.
A verification pass produced 192 element judgements over the retained artifacts
of the second block.  Every episode exited zero and no withheld target text
appeared in any workspace.

\subsection{The first primary endpoint was falsified}

The first deterministic endpoint scored an artifact by matching cue patterns for
each required design element.  Adversarial probes constructed in the
planted-shortcut style \citep{baitbench2026} falsified it: sentences that named
a requirement while denying it were counted as satisfying that requirement in
$96.9\%$ of probes, and paraphrases that satisfied a requirement without its cue
words were missed in $90.9\%$ of probes.  Adding a negation guard reduced the
first error to $0\%$ and raised the second to $100\%$, so the failure is
structural rather than cosmetic.  This is the failure mode in which a correct
surface is reached without the substance it is supposed to indicate
\citep{solutionhacking2026}.

The endpoint was therefore replaced.  Matching was demoted to a candidate
retrieval step and element satisfaction became a verified judgement over the
retrieved span, following grounded checklist scoring \citep{checklistcredit2026}.
On the same retained artifacts the falsified and replacement endpoints correlate
at $r = 0.043$ with a mean absolute difference of $0.484$, confirming outside the
synthetic probes that they do not measure the same construct.  Construct validity
is the load-bearing property here, not score magnitude \citep{constructvalidity2026}.

\subsection{Reliability of the replacement, measured before calibration}

Reliability was measured before requesting any human label.  On 64 stratified
element judgements, re-running the same judge with the same prompt reproduced
the verdict in $84.4\%$ of pairs, and a second judge model agreed in $70.3\%$,
which is $\kappa = 0.411$ after chance correction.  Above a confidence of $0.9$
these rise to $88.0\%$ and $92.0\%$, and none of the 20 high-confidence pairs
disagreed across models, while the intermediate band falls to $56.8\%$, close to
chance.  A reliability floor at $0.9$ therefore abstains before calibration.

This is reliability and not validity.  Agreement among judges, or among repeated
samples of one judge, is a weak predictor of correctness and coexists with
confident error \citep{agreementaudit2026}, and judge validation that rests on
uncorrected agreement does not establish validity \citep{reliabilitywithoutvalidity2026}.
No verdict is admitted as a score until a blinded human-anchored calibration set
certifies an error bound.

\subsection{Design consequences drawn from the measurements}

Two design parameters were fixed by measurement rather than convention.  The
calibration set size follows from the guarantee itself: at $95\%$ confidence a
flawless set of 25 labels certifies a $10\%$ risk level, 11 certifies $20\%$, and
52 certifies $5\%$.  The split of a fixed episode budget between tasks and
repeats was recomputed on the replacement endpoint, where the residual component
is $83.8\%$, the task component $16.2\%$, and the condition and interaction
components are estimated at zero; with a zero interaction component the standard
error of a condition mean no longer depends on that split, so the budget is
allocated for task breadth and the projected paired minimum detectable effect is
about $0.081$.

\subsection{What these results do not support}

They support no claim about structured research state, dynamic retrieval, or any
harness advantage.  Four preregistered decisions were superseded or falsified by
these measurements, and they are recorded as such rather than revised silently,
following the practice of reporting deviations in preregistered agent
experiments \citep{prereg2026}.  The pilot tasks are development data and are
permanently excluded from confirmation because the deterministic checks were
specified after observing their artifacts.  Variance components rest on 32
episodes with few degrees of freedom and a boundary estimate at zero.  All
element verdicts remain inadmissible as scores.


\subsection{Threats to validity and limitations}

The current manuscript establishes neither a structured-state effect nor a
harness-performance gain.  The proposed task source is paper-derived and may
favor re-discovery over genuinely new research.  Target-paper rubrics can anchor
open-ended scoring, but ResearchClawBench notes that report-centered rubrics miss
fine-grained process quality and cannot independently validate novel conclusions
\citep{researchclawbench2026}.

Model and evaluator dependence are additional threats.  A research agent and an
LLM-based judge may share blind spots; literature retrieval may favor accessible
or fashionable methods; and the root agent may possess prior knowledge of public
tasks.  Filesystem isolation, condition-blind scoring, deterministic redlines,
and human calibration reduce but do not eliminate these risks.  Prompt-level
omission of targets is not a leakage boundary.

The minimum pilot has four tasks and one clean run per condition.  It can reveal
instrument failure, leakage, schema mismatch, and gross cost asymmetry, but it
cannot estimate a stable effect.  Confirmatory size remains unspecified until a
disjoint pilot supplies paired discordance, stochastic variance, task-family
correlation, and evaluator disagreement.  Finally, a positive Study A effect
would support experimental-design state under the tested model and tasks; it
would not establish end-to-end autonomous discovery, durable harness
optimization, or cross-domain generality.

\subsection{Measuring episode cost, and an instrument that was wrong}

The pilot recorded token accounting as unmeasured and retained wall-clock duration as
a proxy. Replacing that proxy took four cycles, three of which produced numbers that
were later voided. The sequence is reported because the failures are the result.

A structured output mode and a committed parser first replaced the proxy. Probes then
suggested a fixed context floor, and a decision was recorded to report marginal tokens
against that constant. Repeating the measurement on real episodes falsified it: the
probes had run in an empty directory and measured harness context alone, while a real
episode also carries its mounted workspace. Repeats then falsified the next decision in
turn, showing that a single episode is a draw rather than a value.

The fourth cycle found that the instrument itself had been wrong throughout. The parser
summarised a run by its last usage record, on the assumption that usage accumulated.
Usage is reported per completed API call, and a multi-turn episode re-sends its context
on every call, so the last record describes one call rather than the run. On one
episode this understated cost by a factor of $14.4$ and output tokens by $13.0$. The
fixture written alongside the parser asserted that the last record was the total, so
the suite passed while the instrument was wrong: a fixture that encodes the author's
assumption tests the assumption rather than the system. Two further defects surfaced
during the repair. Transcripts captured through standard output were truncated, losing
records from the middle of four of six runs, so transcripts are now written to files
and the parser reports whether each is complete. And the first repair swallowed every
record after a truncation, which a failing-first fixture caught before it was used.

Every cost figure from the three preceding cycles is therefore void, and the six
episodes were re-measured from complete transcripts. The corrected figures are given in
Table~\ref{tab:cost}. Context dominates both conditions because it is re-sent on each
call, and the full condition made about three times as many calls for about three times
the tokens.

Three limits are attached to this table. The two conditions differ in both factors at
once with three repeats each, so nothing here attributes cost to retrieval or to the
scaffold separately; one run scores one implementation and cannot separate an idea from
its realisation \citep{onerunlottery2026}. Both conditions vary substantially, which is
consistent with reports that agents show large run-to-run variance under identical
tasks, tools, and environment \citep{runvariance2026} and that model outputs are not
deterministic for a fixed prompt \citep{llmrandomness2026}. And dollar cost is excluded
from comparison because caching alters money without altering work
\citep{cachecost2026}, while provider totals can conceal different accounting
\citep{tokenaccounting2026}; cost belongs beside the endpoint rather than behind it
\citep{compressioncost2026}, and any future effect claim from this design must be

agent cost is carried differently by its components \citep{promptwaste2026}.

\subsection{Re-deriving the recorded results}

Every number in the executed blocks had been produced by instrument code that was not
yet audited, so the recorded results were re-derived from the retained artifacts using
the audited instruments. Re-running the blocks would have spent frozen tasks without
testing the recorded numbers at all.

Both blocks re-derived exactly. The variance block returned $32$ episodes, $0$ canary
leaks, $0$ fabrication redlines and $26$ episodes with structural gaps, and the pilot
block returned $16$, $0$, $0$ and $13$, each matching what was recorded. An independent
scan of every retained design and state artifact against all eight per-task withheld
canaries, read from the task bundles rather than from the receipts, also found no leak.

Judged element verdicts are excluded from this check, because they depend on a model
call and cannot be re-derived deterministically.

The judged layer was then measured directly rather than left unexamined. Twenty-four
recorded element judgements were sampled with a fixed seed, stratified across the three
confidence bands, and re-verified from the same retained artifacts with the same judge
and the committed verifier. Eighteen of twenty-four replayed. The split by band is the
result rather than the total: $10$ of $10$ above $0.9$ confidence, $8$ of $10$ between
$0.7$ and $0.9$, and $0$ of $4$ below $0.7$. Two of the twenty-four retrieved no
candidate span, so the verifier returns a negative verdict without any model call; those
are reported separately and excluding them leaves $16$ of $22$.

This supports the $0.9$ admission floor with a measurement rather than with prior
reasoning. A first reading of the low band, that its verdicts carry no reproducible
content, was too strong and the next cycle corrected it. Repeating every low-confidence
item five times gave a mean modal share of $0.88$ with two of five unanimous, against
$1.00$ and four of four for high-confidence controls. The items therefore carry a stable
answer and are labelable. What fails is the recorded draw: it matches the item's own
modal answer in only $1$ of $5$ cases, and it returned an unclear verdict three times
where repeats converge on a definite one. The three low-confidence items stay in the
calibration set, and any recorded low-confidence verdict is replaced by a modal verdict
over at least five repeats before it is compared with a human label.

The scope of that rule was then measured rather than assumed, because the middle band
holds $112$ of the $192$ judgements. Ten middle-band items over five repeats gave a mean
modal share of $0.86$, close to the low band's $0.88$, so within-item stability does not
separate the bands. What separates them is whether the recorded draw represents the
item: $9$ of $10$ in the middle band against $1$ of $5$ in the low band. Reported
confidence therefore predicts whether a single draw represents the item, not how
concentrated the item's answer distribution is, and the rule stays scoped below $0.7$.
Applying it rewrote five records and changed four verdicts. Every unclear verdict in the
whole pool proved to be a low-confidence single draw, and all three resolved to a
definite verdict under repeats, so that category is now empty. Each original draw is
retained beside its replacement.

This correction is recorded with its counterevidence rather than as a settled
improvement. Majority vote has been shown to reduce per-problem accuracy on most hard
problems for small models \citep{majorityhurts2026}, and agreement can be high while the
answer is wrong \citep{wrongconsensus2026}. The rule was applied precisely to the hardest
items, so aggregation could entrench a wrong answer rather than recover a right one. Its
falsifier is written to revert it: if the human labels agree more often with the original
single draws than with the modal verdicts, the correction made the record worse.

Because four verdicts changed, the verified endpoint was recomputed. The recomputation
was first validated against the recorded numbers, and that step immediately paid: the
recomputed minimum detectable effect disagreed with the record, and deriving the quantity
showed the record was right and the check wrong by a factor of $\sqrt{2}$, because a
paired difference of two condition means carries that factor. Once corrected, the
recomputation reproduced every recorded value. On the corrected record the task share of
variance rises from $16.2\%$ to $21.5\%$ and the residual falls from $83.8\%$ to
$78.5\%$, while the condition and interaction components remain exactly zero, so the
structural conclusion that the standard error does not depend on how a budget is split
between tasks and repeats is unchanged. The paired minimum detectable effect moves from
$0.0808$ to $0.0825$, and the correlation between the falsified cue endpoint and the
verified endpoint falls from $0.043$ to $0.018$. No conclusion in this paper depends on
those differences, and the endpoint remains inadmissible for scoring until human labels
exist. Four changed verdicts out of $192$ is a small perturbation, and this says nothing
about how the endpoint would behave under a larger correction; evaluation of this kind is
noisy enough that multiple runs and ordering effects must be reported rather than assumed
away \citep{multiplerunsorder2026}.

Every endpoint number above is produced by a committed script with twenty-two fixtures,
so a reader can re-derive it rather than trust it. The fixtures are of three kinds:
analytic cases whose variance components follow by construction, guard cases that must
raise with the correct cause rather than report a clean zero, and a regression case that
reproduces the earlier published receipt from the original record, using an oracle that
existed before the script. Two conventions that would otherwise be implicit are stated in
the code, since implicit assumptions of exactly that kind are a known cause of analysis
failing to reproduce \citep{implicitassumptions2026}: coverage counts only an exactly
satisfied verdict, and the effect uses the paired difference standard error. A mutation
audit of the script caught five of five injected faults, after one guard fixture was
strengthened to assert why an error was raised rather than only that one was.

Extending the same path check beyond experiment receipts exposed a larger provenance
problem. This checkout is a linked worktree whose shared exclude list blocks the paper
directory, so files are tracked only when staged explicitly. Under that rule $911$ paper
files were tracked while none of the upstream source archives were, which means every
recorded archive path was absent from a clean checkout. The resolution is to treat those
archives as external re-fetchable artifacts rather than committed bytes: the quoted
evidence itself is committed as extracted text for all $114$ archives, and each archive
record must carry a fetch address and a digest. Re-fetching three archives and one PDF
reproduced the recorded digests exactly. This is claim-evidence traceability of the kind
that becomes necessary once claims are drafted faster than they can be checked
\citep{evidenceledger2026}, and its limit is stated: a re-fetch depends on the upstream
service still serving that exact version, so the digest proves identity when a fetch
succeeds and cannot substitute for the bytes if it does not.

A last consistency check turned on the document itself. The abstract still asserted
that the pilot and confirmatory study had not been executed, while the conclusions of
the same document reported three executed blocks, and it named the endpoint that had
since been falsified. A summary can drift away from the results it summarises in the
same way that generated statistical prose drifts numerical values and inverts effect
directions \citep{claimlocked2026}, so the repair is mechanical rather than editorial.
The abstract was rewritten against the executed record, and six checks now forbid
non-execution phrasing while executed receipts exist and read the headline counts from
those receipts rather than from the prose, which is claim verification against a
recorded evidence source \citep{factcheckstages2026}. Three failing-first mutations all
fired: reintroducing the non-execution claim, removing the pilot episode count, and
altering the reported false-positive rate. The gate checks only the numbers it is given,
so an unbound new claim would still pass.

The gate written for this was itself wrong first. It scanned a character window around
each reference, which let a record satisfy the check by borrowing a neighbouring record's
address and digest. A failing-first test that stripped one record did not fire, and the
check was rewritten to walk the parsed structure so the address and digest must sit in
the same record. The same test then fired. Annotation variance
can come from the item or from the rater \citep{annotationvariance2026}, and this
separates the two for these items; but agreement across repeats measures how
concentrated an answer distribution is, not whether its mode is right
\citep{agreementcorrectness2026}. Three limits are attached. Replay is not correctness, since no human
label exists for any replayed item. A disagreement cannot be attributed to judge
stochasticity rather than to item ambiguity from this design
\citep{judgeidentification2026}, which is why no cause is assigned. And a fixed
configuration returning a fixed answer is an assumption that has been refuted elsewhere
\citep{repeataudit2026}, so the low band is treated as unusable rather than as noisy but
informative. That boundary is stated rather than
implied: an artifact that runs is not the same as a claim that can be reproduced
\citep{runnableverifiable2026}, and replaying historical claims from a frozen collection
commonly stops before the claim is reachable \citep{claimreplay2026}.

The re-derivation tool was itself defective on first use. Its filename pattern required
a repeat suffix, so it matched nothing in the pilot block and reported zero leaks and
zero redlines, which reads as a pass. The pattern was widened, an empty match now raises
instead of returning a clean zero, and a fixture covers that case. Five mutations were
then injected into the tool and all five were caught.

\subsection{Auditing the instruments by mutation}

Finding one instrument wrong while its suite passed made the other suites suspect, so
they were audited by injecting faults rather than by reading. Seventeen semantic
mutations were applied one at a time to six instrument modules, each paired suite was
run, and each module was restored and its digest checked.

Thirteen mutations were caught and four survived. Three survivors were in scoring: an
episode with a dimension scored zero could pass as fatal-error-free, a fabrication
redline could stop blocking that endpoint, and the carry-through ratio could exceed one.
The fourth was more serious. The release sandbox could report a workspace as admissible
while a probe had fired, neutralising the whole fail-closed admission gate, and every
test still passed. Five fixtures were added, and the same seventeen mutations were then
all caught.

The audit also exposed a defect in the test harness itself: the scoring suite computed
its pass tally before the newly added checks ran, so it reported fourteen of eighteen
while every check passed. The tally is now computed after all checks.

A following cycle gave the three unaudited modules suites of their own and held them
to the same standard: expected values fixed from each module's stated contract, then
twenty-one further mutations injected. Twenty were caught. The twenty-first was shown by
executing both variants side by side to be an equivalent mutant, because the branch it
changed cannot produce a different output once the evidence directory is absent, so no
fixture was added to force a difference that does not exist. One of the new fixtures was
itself wrong in the same way as before: it asserted that the judge's model selector is
shell quoted, when a safe selector is correctly passed through unquoted, and it was
replaced by a check on an unsafe selector. For the judge module no reliable oracle exists
\citep{oracleavailability2026}, so its suite asserts the deterministic shell around the
model through an injected runner and makes no live call, following the practice of
expressing behavioural contracts as relations rather than expected values
\citep{metamorphicrelations2026}.

This is why the audit used mutation rather than review. An oracle that takes its
expected value from the system it judges cannot fail, because a fault moves measurement
and expectation together \citep{oracleanchoring2026}, which is exactly how the cost
fixture protected a wrong parser. A suite can also hold high coverage while leaving a
documented behaviour unvalidated \citep{coveragebehaviour2026}, and silent measurement
failure is common enough in reported incidents to treat as a primary risk rather than an
edge case \citep{evalblindness2026}. Two limits are recorded. Catching seventeen
mutations does not show the instruments are correct, only that these faults are
detected. After the following cycle no
instrument module lacks a paired suite, but a mutation set chosen by the author of the
code can still miss a fault class that neither the code nor the mutations consider.

\subsection{A census of the project's own decision record}

Because the method above claims a loop that corrects itself, that claim is
measured rather than asserted. A committed script reads the decision ledger and
counts records, falsifiers, and later revisions; eight fixtures cover the count,
the rate, the empty-ledger case, and the rule that only round-numbered record
lists are read \citep{seqmeta2026}. Measured against the ledger digest recorded in
the receipt, the project had made $28$ recorded decisions in $15$ recording
groups. Every record carried a falsifier written before the result was known, and
$20$ carried executed evidence. Three decisions were later revised: one endpoint
falsified by measurement, one allocation voided as a consequence, and one
superseded. The revision rate is $0.107$.

Two limits are stated with the number. First, the census counts the ledger that
records the decision to run it, so it is reported against a stated digest rather
than as a constant. Second, this is a descriptive census of a single project with
no comparison group, so it cannot show that writing falsifiers caused the
revisions; it shows only that revisions happened and what triggered each one. All
three were triggered by the project's own executed measurements rather than by
outside review. This is the kind of process evidence that evaluation beyond final
scores calls for \citep{beyondfinalscores2026}, and it is also the boundary of what
such evidence establishes: gates and receipts are operational rigor, which
substitutes for understanding rather than supplying it \citep{rigorai2026}.

\section{Conclusions}

This thesis set out to test whether a persistent, harnessed LLM-agent system
designs and revises empirical research better than information-matched
alternatives.  It did not reach that question.  It reached the prior question of
whether the instrument that would answer it can be trusted, and the honest
finding is that the first such instrument could not.

Three blocks were executed.  A 16-episode instrument pilot showed that the
hidden-task boundary held and that fabrication redlines had no discriminating
power on competent artifacts.  A 32-episode block supplied variance components.
A verification pass produced 192 element judgements.  Along the way the first
primary endpoint was falsified by its own preregistered test, replaced by
retrieval plus verification, and the replacement was measured for stability
before any human label was requested.  Four preregistered decisions were
superseded or voided by these measurements and are recorded as such.

The contribution is therefore methodological rather than empirical.  It is a
research loop in which each measuring instrument carries an explicit falsifier,
is tested against adversarial probes before use, and is discarded when it fails;
in which every design claim re-derives from committed bytes; and in which the
system that produces a number is deliberately separated from the system that
verifies it, because self-authored verification is unreliable when one agent owns
both sides \citep{verifiergap2026}.  Diagnostic evaluation of autonomous research
agents points the same way: what is informative is where a pipeline breaks, not a
single terminal score \citep{autoresearchfail2026}.

Three limits bound this claim.  The design-quality endpoint is scored without
executing any design, so construct validity rests on the element checklists
rather than on outcomes.  Judged verdicts remain inadmissible until a blinded
human-anchored calibration set certifies an error bound, and that set is the one
component this system cannot produce for itself.  The nine-area architecture
remains a roadmap: only the connected slice needed to identify the treatment was
built, and the remaining areas are design-only.

Two directions follow.  The immediate one is to collect the calibration labels,
admit or reject judged scoring on the certified bound, and only then run the
confirmatory block on tasks disjoint from every task used here.  The longer one
is to measure what verification costs, since reliability in practice depends on
the effort required to check an output rather than on correctness alone
\citep{verificationcost2026}; this project spent 192 model calls to verify 32
artifacts, and a research system that cannot account for that cost has not
finished describing itself.
