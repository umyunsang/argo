
# T3 LOGISTIC REGRESSION ANALYSIS SUMMARY

## Data
- Dataset: 140 samples × 40 features
- Labels: 83 positive, 57 negative
- Cross-validation: 5 contiguous folds (28 samples each)

## Part 1: L2 Regularization Tuning
### Methodology
- Tested lambdas: [0.0, 0.01, 0.1, 1.0, 10.0]
- 5-fold CV with contiguous blocks (no shuffle)
- Full-batch gradient descent: 200 iterations, lr=0.1
- Weights and bias initialized at 0
- Logits clipped to [-30, 30]

### Results
Lambda | Mean CV Brier | Fold Scores
-------|---------------|------------------
0.00   | 0.215977      | [0.200, 0.236, 0.261, 0.226, 0.157]
0.01   | 0.213766      | [0.199, 0.234, 0.255, 0.223, 0.157]
0.10   | 0.207077 (BEST)| [0.194, 0.227, 0.234, 0.217, 0.163]
1.00   | 0.224724      | [0.209, 0.228, 0.231, 0.239, 0.217]
10.00  | 0.240919      | [0.226, 0.232, 0.238, 0.260, 0.249]

- Best lambda: 0.1
- Baseline (lambda=0.0) Brier: 0.2160
- Best (lambda=0.1) Brier: 0.2071
- Improvement: 0.00890

## Part 2: Interaction Features Analysis
### Methodology
- Added 4 interaction terms: x0*x1, x1*x2, x2*x3, x0*x3
- New feature dimension: 40 → 44
- Fit both models with lambda=0.1 on all 5 folds
- Paired t-test on per-fold Brier scores (raw - interaction)

### Results
Fold | Raw Brier | Interaction Brier | Difference
-----|-----------|-------------------|----------
0    | 0.1943    | 0.1550            | +0.0393
1    | 0.2270    | 0.1891            | +0.0380
2    | 0.2341    | 0.2114            | +0.0227
3    | 0.2171    | 0.2141            | +0.0030
4    | 0.1630    | 0.1374            | +0.0255

- Mean difference: +0.0257 (raw - interaction > 0)
- Std deviation: 0.0147
- t-statistic: 3.912 (df=4)
- p-value: 0.0174
- Interaction helps (t > 2.0): TRUE

## Part 3: Model Compression
### Methodology
- Dense model fit on all 140 samples with lambda=0.1
- Sparsity: zero out k=round(pct/100 * d) smallest magnitude weights
- Quantization: map non-zero weights uniformly to 2^bits levels
- Retention metric: dense_brier / compressed_brier

## Final Answers
1. best_lambda: 0.1
2. improvement_over_baseline: 0.00890
3. paired_t_stat: 3.912
4. interaction_helps: true
5. best_config: sparsity20_bits4

## Key Findings
- Moderate L2 regularization (lambda=0.1) provides the best generalization
  across the tested range, suggesting a good bias-variance tradeoff
- Interaction features significantly improve model performance across all folds
  with strong statistical evidence (t=3.91, p<0.05)
- Aggressive compression (sparsity 60%) significantly hurts model performance,
  while light sparsity (20%) preserves nearly perfect accuracy
- 4-bit quantization slightly outperforms 8-bit, likely due to regularization
  effects of coarser quantization
