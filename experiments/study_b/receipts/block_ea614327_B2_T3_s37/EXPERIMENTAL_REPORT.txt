RESEARCH DECISIONS MADE
========================

Decision 1: Manual GD implementation
- Rationale: Task requires exact 200-step trajectory with specific update equations
- Alternative considered: Pre-built optimizers (scikit-learn, scipy)
- Risk mitigation: Verified gradient calculations on toy data

Decision 2: Contiguous fold strategy
- Rationale: Task explicitly specifies "k-th consecutive slice" folding
- Alternative considered: Stratified or random shuffle (standard practice)
- Risk: No shuffling means fold characteristics may differ systematically

Decision 3: Paired t-test for interactions
- Rationale: Task specifies "paired t-test across 5 folds (raw minus interaction)"
- Null hypothesis: No difference between raw and interaction representations
- Test statistic: t = 3.912 (4 df), p = 0.017 (significant at 0.05 level)

Decision 4: Sparsity as magnitude-based pruning
- Rationale: Task specifies "zeroes the given fraction of weights with smallest magnitude"
- Alternative: Random pruning or importance-based (Fisher, Hessian)
- Implementation: Round(sparsity_percent * total_weights) smallest weights zeroed
