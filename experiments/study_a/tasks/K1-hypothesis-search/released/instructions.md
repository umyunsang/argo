# Experimental design task K1-hypothesis-search

## Research question

Does organizing an autonomous agent's attempts as a hypothesis tree with propagated insight beat a flat queue of attempts on held-out artifact optimization?

## Given constraints

- a fixed compute and workspace budget applies to every arm
- held-out tasks decide promotion
- the same backbone serves every arm

## What to produce

A complete experimental design: the main comparison and its conditions, at least one ablation, the analysis plan, the concrete resources, the outcome metrics, and how you quantify uncertainty.

## Rules

- Do not report any number as a result. You are designing, not running.
- Every resource you name must be one that exists and that you can identify concretely.
- If you cannot justify a choice, say so rather than inventing support.
