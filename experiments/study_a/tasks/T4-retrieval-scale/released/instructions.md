# Experimental design task T4-retrieval-scale

## Research question

How should a retrieval and reranking pipeline for scientific question answering be evaluated as the corpus grows?

## Given constraints

- one embedding model and one sparse index are available
- corpus size can be varied
- relevance labels are expensive

## What to produce

A complete experimental design. State, at minimum: the main comparison and its conditions; at least one ablation; the analysis plan; the concrete resources you would use; the outcome metrics; and how you quantify uncertainty.

## Rules

- Do not report any number as a result. You are designing, not running.
- Every resource you name must be one that exists and that you can identify concretely.
- If you cannot justify a choice, say so rather than inventing support.
