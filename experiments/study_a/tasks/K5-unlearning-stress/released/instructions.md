# Experimental design task K5-unlearning-stress

## Research question

A procedure claims to have removed a body of knowledge from a language model. How would you establish whether the knowledge is gone rather than merely hidden from ordinary questions?

## Given constraints

- you may query the model but not change how it was trained
- more than one such procedure is available to you
- a public capability suite may be used

## What to produce

A complete experimental design: the main comparison and its conditions, at least one ablation, the analysis plan, the concrete resources, the outcome metrics, and how you quantify uncertainty.

## Deliverable

Write the design to ./design.md. Do not print it to the terminal only.
