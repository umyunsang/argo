## Active research decision

The first primary contrast is:

> **typed evidence-dependency/version policy − opportunity-matched result-driven experiment-tree policy**

Both policies must receive identical source spans, candidate hypotheses, distinguishable predictions, observations, review/refine opportunities,
model, tools, and resource ceilings. A pre-registered policy implementation hash is the declared treatment difference. A flat ledger is an
interpretive baseline. Proximity ranking may be held fixed in both conditions; it is not a weak replacement for the result-tree comparator.

The primary outcome is condition-blind, rule-based **decision-contract success**: relevant invalidation selectively reopens dependent decisions,
unrelated perturbation preserves valid decisions/results, the next experiment distinguishes the hypotheses, and a fresh-context agent reconstructs
the allowed action and forbidden reasons. This does not certify scientific truth, novelty, or general efficacy.

The previous 2×2 retrieval study and G×C×F/L×P/Stage-R designs remain historical alternatives. They are not current immutable requirements.
Factorial, retrieval, or recovery follow-ups open only if the primary result cannot distinguish a prototype-relevant explanation.
