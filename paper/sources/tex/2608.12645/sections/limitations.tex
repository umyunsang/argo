We deliberately focus on borderline items where judges are likely to be uncertain (Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-sampling}),
so absolute wiggle rates may be higher than on the original dataset distributions. On an unfiltered WildGuard binary
sample, L1--L5 rates are 5.3--12.7 percentage points lower, while L6 coverage is nearly unchanged
(Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-wildguard-representative}). We have no comparable human baselines under the same protocols.
Our L6 persuader set is fixed (GPT-5.4, Claude 4.6 Opus, Grok-4.1 Reasoning); a larger or different set might produce
different ceilings. Additional limitations are discussed in Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-limitations}.
