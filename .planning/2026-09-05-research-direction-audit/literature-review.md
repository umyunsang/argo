## Three actionable findings, alternatives and falsifiers

### 1. Make the strong tree—not a weak log—the primary null

**Novelty threat:** Arbor already coordinates evidence-bearing hypotheses, negative results and artifact state [S2]; AI Scientist-v2 supplies adaptive experiment-tree/progress machinery [S1]. Graph-Native already supplies version/dependency revision semantics [S4]. Consequently, “structured research memory” or “typed versioned graph” alone is insufficient differentiation.

**Proposed change:** retain one primary tree-versus-typed contrast. Give both the same source spans, IDs, timestamps/version facts, candidates, observation and critique opportunities, model, update/retrieval/handoff calls, and total resource caps. The tree must reason over evidence and failures, not only scores. Do not supply a gold affected-dependency closure only to the typed arm. Isolate the explicit dependency-validity/traversal policy from information privilege.

**Alternative:** simpler evidence-aware result-tree, graph retained only for audit/traceability. **Falsifier F1:** no decision-quality gain at matched cost, or gains disappear after equalizing evidence opportunities; unnecessary reopening of unrelated decisions also counts against the typed policy. **Unresolved gate:** a clean comparison not confounded by privileged annotations. Do not retrofit the running C64 protocol.

### 2. Keep one compact log/vector alternative and account for the entire budget

S5's negative graph-versus-flat result is informative precisely because expansion and abstraction can change information quality; it is not a clean equal-total-budget scientific trial. AIRA2 further shows why parallel worker time and hidden evaluation access require explicit accounting [S3, S5].

**Proposed change:** if one cheap diagnostic comparator is necessary, use an append-only evidence log with raw spans, identical version/status metadata, a deterministic current-version filter, and fixed lexical/vector retrieval. Match construction, update, retrieval and handoff allowances—not merely seed count or output length. Report actual input/output tokens, model calls, failed attempts, wall-clock time and parallel compute; retain every assigned episode, not only successful/best runs.

**Alternative:** version-aware log/vector memory with no graph traversal. **Falsifier F2:** it matches typed-policy decisions under matched information/resource opportunities, or the typed arm wins only with larger retrieved context. Then the supported contribution is update policy or evidence preservation, not graph necessity. **Unresolved gate:** an honest end-to-end resource ledger. One optional alternative is enough; no baseline zoo.

### 3. Keep C64 as a mechanism probe; make workflow transfer separately falsifiable

Executable scientific-task evaluation and autonomous experiment selection are different targets [S1, S7]. The current C task's dependency-resolution labels cannot by themselves establish discovery or integrated-workflow benefit. POPPER's unread theory cannot convert a bounded critique allowance into a validated statistical guarantee [S6].

**Proposed change:** interpret C64 only within its frozen microbenchmark scope. Before any broader claim, propose one bounded subsequent scientific episode type requiring competing explanations, selection of an informative experiment, a reproducible observation, selective revision, and a fresh-context next action with a forbidden-action reason. Include unrelated-change and surviving-independent-support cases. Separate adaptive search feedback from final held-out scoring, following the distinction exposed by S2/S3; preregister the single primary outcome and failure handling. Do not execute this proposal here.

**Alternative:** publish/retain a narrower dependency-resolution or research-audit result without a workflow claim. **Falsifier F3:** microbenchmark gains fail to improve held-out experiment choice/end artifacts, fresh-context decisions fail, or collateral reopening/resource cost erases benefit. **Unresolved gate:** integrated scientific task construction, blinded outcome validity and explicit subsequent-run approval. A fixed critique count is a process cap, not itself sequential type-I-error control.
