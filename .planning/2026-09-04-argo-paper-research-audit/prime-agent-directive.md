## Fixed research contribution

Use this public-facing research question:

> Under a fixed model, tool surface, corpus snapshot, and resource budget, do typed evidence-and-experiment lineage, design competition with rule-based admission, and a bounded falsification/refinement loop improve the validity, scientific task outcome, reproducibility, and recovery of long-horizon autonomous R&D?

ARGO is a **lineage-aware, evidence-grounded, recovery-capable closed-loop R&D harness**. It is not a bundle of product names and does not claim invention of persistent REPLs, recursive agents, semantic search, evidence graphs, or multi-agent review.

Classify the benchmark inspirations as follows:

- Fixed inherited substrate: Prime Agent persistent IPython/RLM/daemon/session recovery and Continual Harness facilities.
- Fixed external lifecycle oracle: OpenResearch branch/run/log/artifact receipts; claim only behavior actually verified from pinned code or receipts.
- Retrieval adapter: Exa or another semantic search provider; product documentation proves interface existence, not scientific benefit.
- Minimal external anchor: Pi-style one-shot/minimal-tool behavior; product documentation is implementation provenance, not efficacy evidence.
- Testable ARGO mechanisms: `G` typed evidence/experiment lineage, `C` design competition and deterministic admission, `F` bounded falsification/refinement.

The public paper may cite only full-read academic primary sources for scientific claims. Pi, OpenResearch, and Exa product materials belong in the internal implementation/disclosure ledger unless an archival paper has been fully read and claim-located. Prime Agent's preprint is inherited-system self-description, not an independent efficacy comparison.

## Main confirmatory design: 2 x 2 x 2

Use a complete factorial design with all eight `G x C x F` cells. Do not call the result globally optimal. The maximum allowed wording, if supported, is **best observed among the eight preregistered configurations under the evaluated task distribution and budget**.

### Factors

- `G=1`: persistent typed evidence and experiment lineage with immutable protocol/run/artifact identity, source spans, explicit support/contradiction, and separate research-state versus engine-version lineage. `G=0`: flat append-only notes containing the same accessible content but no typed edges, graph gates, or lineage operations.
- `C=1`: two independently generated, mechanism-distinct designs; an independent critic may explain defects; a preregistered deterministic rule admits/revises/blocks and records rejected alternatives. `C=0`: one design, no tournament or alternative selection.
- `F=1`: at most three preregistered falsification/refinement rounds, with early stop after two consecutive improvements below the minimum threshold or when the budget is exhausted. `F=0`: one-shot design/execution/review with no revision.

`G0C0F0` is the same-backbone single-agent causal reference. A model-only/no-tool anchor may be measured in development work, but it is not a substitute for the same-backbone reference.

Every cell must use the identical model checkpoint/API revision, system-task interface, tool permissions, retrieval policy and corpus cutoff, environment image, maximum token/tool/time/cost budget, hidden task packet, scorer, retry rule, and stopping authority. Randomize and counterbalance cell order within task. Any treatment difference not declared in the treatment manifest is a protocol failure.

If the full factorial is unaffordable after the preregistered power and cost simulation, do not silently reduce it. Use `FULL`, `-G`, `-C`, `-F`, and `G0C0F0` only and downgrade the claim to **leave-one-mechanism-out removal impacts**; no interaction or optimum claim is then permitted.

## Staged evaluation program

### Stage 0: measurement certification; zero-cost before any episode

Repair specifications and add deterministic negative/positive fixtures for:

- outer seed passed to the runner and `inner_seed == outer_seed`;
- unique task ID and task-content hash;
- evaluator crash, timeout, nonzero exit, missing output, and parse failure => score zero and inadmissible execution, never a valid answer;
- pinned scorer environment and exact dependency versions;
- arm loader and single-component manipulation checks;
- hard token/tool/time/cost ceilings;
- condition-blind scorer;
- OS-level oracle isolation and access logging, not path-name inspection;
- receipt provenance: origin, model/provider revision, protocol fingerprint, harness commit, task hash, command, environment hash, transcript/artifact paths, timestamps, exit state, usage;
- stale-run detection that distinguishes a live worker from an orphaned registry state.

No paid experiment may be proposed for admission until every Stage 0 fixture passes in a clean environment.

### Stage R: retrieval-policy screening

On a development-only literature task set, compare no retrieval, fixed-budget semantic/vector retrieval, and citation/entity-graph traversal. Measure answer correctness, citation precision/recall, evidence coverage, abstention, latency, token use, and cost. Choose the main-study retrieval policy using only the development set, then freeze the corpus cutoff, index/hash, query budget, and provider version. Do not infer that Exa itself improves science; it is one adapter.

### Stage 1: factorial scientific-task study

- Development/power pilot: at least four distinct tasks, excluded permanently from confirmation.
- Confirmatory set: 12-20 distinct hidden tasks across at least four scientific domains, sampled from the current verified ScienceAgentBench release or an equivalently executable, expert-validated suite.
- Use the task, not a filename seed or rollout, as the population inference unit.
- Run at least three independent, correctly propagated seeds per task-cell only after a cost envelope is shown to and approved by the human.
- If fewer than 12 distinct tasks can pass environment/scorer/oracle certification, block population-level confirmation rather than replacing tasks with repeated rollouts.

### Stage 2: recovery and reproducibility

For representative tasks, inject deterministic interruption at three preregistered points: after planning, during execution, and during review/refinement. Compare checkpoint/lineage enabled versus removed. Measure resume success, lost-work ratio, repeated-action rate, time-to-resume, final-quality delta, and exact protocol/run/artifact digest agreement. Use 6-10 reproducible capsules such as a certified CORE-Bench subset or an equivalent pinned suite.

### Stage 3: integrated cases and NAIS demonstration

Keep three end-to-end cases as qualitative/external-validity demonstrations, not the sample used for causal significance. For NAIS, build the competition artifact during the official event window from allowed public OSS with a separate start timestamp, repository/branch, dependency manifest, and artifact lineage. The thesis harness and Study B code are not the hackathon prototype. The minimum event path is: sources/problem -> two designs -> rule admission -> one deterministic execution -> evidence-bound decision -> one refine step. Add interruption/resume only if the event rules and time allow.

## Preregistered hypotheses

- `H1-G`: `G=1` improves rule-verified evidence/experiment lineage completeness and reduces unsupported retained claims relative to `G=0`.
- `H2-C`: `C=1` improves held-out scientific task score relative to `C=0` under the same maximum resource budget.
- `H3-F`: `F=1` improves method validity and held-out task score relative to `F=0` without exceeding the resource cap.
- `H4-GC`: competition benefits are larger with typed lineage than with flat state.
- `H5-GF`: falsification/refinement is less likely to introduce unsupported or stale conclusions with typed lineage.
- `H6-FULL`: `G1C1F1` has the best observed reliability-adjusted task score among preregistered cells; this claim fails unless adjusted pairwise intervals exclude the minimum practical effect against every comparator.
- `H7-RECOVERY`: separated graph-backed research/engine lineage reduces recovery loss under injected interruption without a final-quality drop beyond a preregistered noninferiority margin.

Do not analyze or change these hypotheses after confirmatory outcomes are visible.

## Outcomes and analysis

Use one primary endpoint: the condition-blind, rule-based, held-out task score defined by the certified task scorer, with fatal protocol violation, oracle access, invalid provenance, or agent-caused execution failure scored zero in the intention-to-run analysis.

Report separately, never hidden inside a composite:

- official task pass/score, pass@1, median, and reliability `pass^3`;
- protocol-valid completion and independent clean rerun success;
- evidence-link completeness requiring both a source span and execution artifact where the claim needs both;
- citation precision/recall, coverage, unsupported-claim rate, and justified abstention;
- duplicate proposal/action rate, resume fidelity, human interventions;
- invalid-run, crash, timeout, provider/infrastructure failure, wall-clock, tokens, cost, tool calls, and experiment count.

Infrastructure incidents may have a preregistered blinded retry rule, but the original attempt remains in the reliability denominator. Report both intention-to-run and protocol-complete sensitivity analyses. A model critic is never the primary oracle; any LLM judge is secondary and must be calibrated on a blinded human-labelled subset.

Estimate factorial contrasts within task. Use task-cluster bootstrap 95% intervals as the robust primary uncertainty analysis; use mixed-effects models with condition fixed and task/reviewer random effects as a secondary model. Predefine a minimum practically important effect and determine the final task count from a disjoint pilot power simulation. Apply Holm correction to the confirmatory hypothesis family. Do not use repeated rollouts from one task as independent `n`.
