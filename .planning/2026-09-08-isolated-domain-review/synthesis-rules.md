# Root synthesis rules

- Treat reviewer outputs as independent-context observations, not votes or experimental ground truth.
- Merge only findings about the same exact object and failure mechanism; retain materially different objections.
- Recheck cited bytes and line ranges; classification distinguishes direct facts, inference, missing evidence and actual contradiction.
- A qualified prospective claim may be sound even if implementation/efficacy is pending. Separate missing future protocol detail from current document defects.
- For each integrated finding record accept / accept_with_qualification / defer_to_task_bound_protocol / reject_with_evidence, the minimal action and preserved boundary.
- Do not reopen already-approved task/start/A/A2 decisions or prescribe new model/compute runs as if review authority permits them.
- Close all worker sessions after their reports are preserved. Review graph is a local audit/decision graph, not the active native research controller.
