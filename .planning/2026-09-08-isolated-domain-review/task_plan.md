# Isolated domain review

## Objective
Use fresh team-agent sessions to independently review the current thesis-to-prototype research across literature/novelty, causal/statistical design, graph/memory control and runtime/prototype ownership. Root integrates agreements and disagreements with evidence.

## Phases
1. Freeze shared review inputs and assign isolated scopes — complete
2. Four independent domain reviews — complete

## Isolation and scope
No history is forked into reviewers. Read-only hashed packet, separate owned output folders, no access to other reviewers' reports until submissions complete. This is context/ownership isolation, not OS/filesystem security isolation. Root+3 concurrency slots; fourth fresh reviewer starts when a slot is free. No native/manuscript/protocol/source changes, model experiments, external publication or credentials access.
