### STAT-02 · HIGH · 단일 hidden artifact primary는 타당하지만 전 배정 분모의 수치 결과와 결측 분석은 과제별로 아직 정의되지 않았다.

## 타당한 부분

- C−B is the compulsory-process package effect and G−C the graph-control package increment; pure topology is explicitly disclaimed. Strong evidence/recovery rights and common scheduler are retained.
- Single controller-selected locked artifact precedes independent hidden scoring; archive-best, best-seed and hidden-feedback reselection are rejected. Current C0 method choice is already specified.
- Programme/source ancestry is the independent unit; contexts, candidates and seeds are nested. P0 exposure keeps its family on the development side, despite zero observed scores.
- All-assigned failure/missingness/cost accounting, no fabricated scores and no post-null promotion of diagnostics are explicitly preserved.
- Current C0 is zero-start feasibility, not a failed efficacy study. A/A2 approval and prepared/content-verified split are not reopened by old generic null/authorization fields.
- A bounded null, negative or inconclusive result may close a study with appropriate scope; it need not produce a graph winner or a positive thesis result.
